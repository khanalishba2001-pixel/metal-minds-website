Our Story

Steel for Zimbabwe

Metal Minds was founded in 2010 to give Zimbabwean contractors access to quality construction steel at fair prices, backed by reliable service.

From a single warehouse in Harare we have grown to supply over 2,400 projects across the country - from residential builds to large commercial developments.

Every product is graded to Zimbabwe Standards Association specifications. Every quote is transparent. Every delivery is on time.
